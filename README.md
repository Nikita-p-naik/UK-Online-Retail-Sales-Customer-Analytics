# UK Online Retail Sales & Customer Analytics

Power BI portfolio project analysing the **UCI Online Retail dataset** to understand sales performance, customer behaviour, product trends, and geographic performance.

### Dashboard Pages

* **Executive Overview** – revenue, customers, orders, loyalty tiers and sales trends.
* **Business Performance** – revenue, orders, product categories and country performance.
* **Geographic & Operational Insights** – countries served, order value, quantity sold and return rates.
* **Product Performance** – top/bottom products, category distribution and category return rates.

### Key KPIs

Total Revenue, Total Customers, Total Orders, Return Rate, Average Order Value, Total Quantity Sold, Average Items per Order, Products and Categories.

### Key Analysis

* Customer segmentation into New, Returning and Loyal customers
* Top and bottom products by sales/revenue
* Revenue and orders by month and country
* Product category performance
* Return-rate analysis by country and category

### Tools Used

**Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Data Visualisation**

### Data Source

**UCI Online Retail Dataset**

### Project Goal

To convert transactional retail data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that helps identify **sales trends, customer value, product performance, and geographic opportunities**.
